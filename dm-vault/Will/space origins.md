# Space Origins

This document serves as an origin story and background for the character named "Space", and his sidekick "OTTO".

---

## STORY - The Desert Origins of Space
Space, at least that’s what they called him, his caravan. They found him in a cavern system that had been exposed from beneath the great desert of Aunoroch. Uncertain at what age he was found, the young Kenku was the sole survivor of the cave in from where he was found. Affixed to his head was a strange device composed of arcane and scientific wonders alike. A large bulbous sphere, a backbone of metallic components and a spherical glass shape affixed to the cent of eight outstretched arms. The machine clamped around young Spaces head, resembling a helmet. Without this machine, there’s little doubt that Space would have survived the cave in, the mixture of dust and debris that initially buried him would have suffocated him within minutes. He awoke days later, having been carried by the caravan, the Tribal Leader decided that the caravan would look after him. And while the Caravan had no other Kenkus in its midst, they were no stranger to different species here. 

Upon awakening, the kenku lashed out, immediately opening his eyes as the machine affixed to his head began to release its grip. Once fully released, the machine began… not quite flying, but floating in the air next to the Kenku. In the wagon alongside Kenku sat a Dwarf and an Earth Genasi, both frozen at the sudden outburst and movement from the Kenku who had laid sleeping for almost 2 days.

“Greetings!” An eerily metallic and cheerful voice rang out. The Genasi and Dwarf looked at the Kenku quizzically. The voice, it didn’t come from the bird like humanoid sitting across from them, no it came from the floating eight limbed machine that floated in the wagon bed slightly above them. “My name is O…T…T…O, I am here with my good friend.” A brief pause as the machine motionlessly levitated in the air, “Well that’s odd, it seems my memory has been damaged or modified, but I can assure you this Kenku here is my master.”
 
“What in the 9 hells am I seeing right now Ravert, is that machine speaking to us?” The Dwarf exclaimed? “It has been quite some time since I’ve seen a machine of this complexity. This thing is a marvel itself, no visible power source, not a magical stone in sight.” As the dwarf observed the floating machination rather closely, the Genasi leaned forward a bit and locked his gaze on the Kenku who had initially been startled, but calmed a bit as the floating device gave the introduction.

“Child, I am Ravert, an earth Genasi native to the Aunorach desert. This is Phel, he is a dwarf. And while he is not from these lands originally he has lived here longer than I have been on this planet.” 
The Kenku stared at Ravert, giving no indication of acknowledgement or any response for that matter. Instead, the machine responded, “You’ll have to forgive my master, initial scans indicate that the blunt force trauma experienced during recent collapse have induced severe Post Traumatic Amnesia. Grade and severity uncertain.”
Phel and Ravert exchanged another stare, this time Phel’s mouth stood agape, “By the steel Ravert, what the hell is this machine talking about. Something about this Kenku being afflicted by some sort of curse?” 

“Not humanoids of medicine I can see,” the machine sputtered out sarcastically. “Nevertheless, I mean to say that this child here does not have a full recount of his memories as of yet. He seems to remember how to move and breathe so that’s a start, the lack of words being spoken is concer-“ The machine was interrupted suddenly as a loud cracking noise came from the wagon just behind them followed shortly after by a shriek of pain. 
As the conversation halted Ravert pulled the curtain that had covered the rear of their wagon cart, revealing a sunbeaten landscape riddled in sand as far as eyes could see. The sight immediately behind them had shown the Cartwright behind them buried face first in the road that dissected the grand desert. Ravert and Phel disembarked from the wagon and rushed over to the fallen Cartwright, as they approached a gruesome scene became clearer. The wagon had failed structurally where the arms affixed to the mounts, causing the driver to be pulled forward out of his seat, the wooden beam from the arm thrust itself clear through the human's leg, the beam currently fixed and protruding from both sides of the man’s legs. Ravert and Phel began to visually panic, “Quick! Notify the Tribal Leader to halt! The bell! Ring the bell!” Ravert ordered Phel. The dwarf stood for a moment hesitating at the loss of blood in the man’s legs before running to the rear of the cart. Seconds later a bell rang, alerting the rest of the caravan to haunt. 3 rings before pausing. Ravert approached the man, offering words of soothing. “It’ll be okay Gwin, we’re getting help.” Ravert eyes furrowed, he had managed to flip Gwin over with a bit of effort.
AS the sun glared down on the reddish gold sand, the wagon that had shifted violently left Gwin pinned to the ground by the beam that stuck clear through his leg. Blood began to pool, soaking the sand beneath. The crowd gathered, the caravan effectively halted due to Phel’s ringing of the alarm bell. An air of hesitation stuck in the thick of the gathered ensemble, Gwin’s fate seemed to be cast, his death imminent. 

“Space!” The Kenku from before blurted out, attempting to get past the crowd. “Space! Space” He repeated as the crowd finally began to back away from the bleeding Gwin. One last time he shouted, “Space!” Both Talon-like hands performing a pushing motion, the Kenku now stood directly next to Gwin. Looking above his shoulder at the floating O.T.T.O, the Kenku pointed at the injured man. 
Quickly adjusting its own form, O.T.T.O. mounted himself directly onto Gwin’s head, fitting itself as a helmet. As this occurred, Ravert took a step forward quickly, “Wait, wha-”
“Space!” The Kenku barked at the man before turning his head back to O.T.T.O.
“Master,” the bot began, “injury risk level priority 1, immediate stabilization required before Foreign Object Removal. Estimated survival chance 90% if stabilization occurs within the next 180 seconds.
A quick nod from the Kenku as he kneeled down next to the man, he pulled back his hood to reveal his eyes that were fixed on the beam lodged in Gwin’s leg, he began muttering under his breath, words only fully heard by him as the crowd watched on, nervously. After a brief paus, the Kenku’s arms began to move swiftly, they became almost a blur as he began to craft a makeshift splint from the nearby cart wreckage. Carefully grasping one hand onto the beam prompted O.T.T.O. to respond “Master, if Foreign Object occurs before stabilization survival probability drops below 20%.” the Kenku nodded, aware of the situation at hand. His hands precisely fashioned the makeshift splint he had managed to piece together to surround Gwin’s leg while he simultaneously tourniqueted blood flow to the leg. He looked back at O.T.T.O. with a somewhat questioning look. “Stabilization complete, removal process optimized. Commence immediately. 
The Kenku turned to Ravert, who stood in front of the crowd, “You, Earth Genasi, grab this beam. When I give the word you are going to pull hard and fast as straight as you can.” Ravert hesitated, before the Kenku’s stern gaze called him into action. He quickly wrapped both of his arms around the beam, Gwin winced in pain as it moved slightly. 
Ravert waited for what felt like minutes, before the Kenku barked, “Pull!” Almost the moment the beam was pulled out, the Kenku pressed his hand that held a bit of the cart’s fabric directly onto Gwin’s punctured leg. Pressing as he began to wrap, he worked quickly each movement serving a purpose. The group had slowly creeped forward, crowding the injured man and Kenku, “Space!” he squawked, correcting them. 
His hands began to work in a blur, before eventually O.T.T.O. let out, “Stabilization verified, blood flow adequate, pain levels remain elevated. Treatment complete.” before releasing his limbs from Gwin’s head and floating back to the Kenku’s side. 
As he exhaled, Kenku wiped his bloodied hands onto the robes. The crowd stilled and murmured, showing their intermittent gratitude, “He saved him.” an onlooker murmured.
“You saved one of ours.” a member of the crowd said, the semi-circle split revealing a human male adorned in a decorative robe. “That skill is no small thing, what is your name child?”
A brief moment passed, the Kenku didn’t respond, the man had asked him for a name that didn’t exist. “Space, Tribal Leader, his name… is Space” Ravert said, interjecting in the Kenku's stead.
