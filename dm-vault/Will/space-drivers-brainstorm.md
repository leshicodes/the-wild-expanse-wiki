## The Breadcrumb Approach

### O.T.T.O.'s Last Message (FINALIZED)

O.T.T.O.'s lights flicker. The hum that has followed Space for as long as he can remember begins to fade.

Then, steady - almost tender:

> *"Go to the stars, little bird. They're waiting for you."*

A pause. Something shifts behind the glass of O.T.T.O.'s central eye. And then - a laugh. Soft. Not quite O.T.T.O.'s. Warmer. The sound of someone finally letting go. The sound of a long breath released.

The kind of laugh you'd hear from a parent watching their child stand on their own for the first time - pride and ache and relief, all tangled together.

Then dark.

**Why this works:**
- "Little bird" = affectionate, implies a longer relationship than Space remembers
- "The stars" = points toward Spelljammer/Wildspace without naming the Academy
- "They're waiting" = someone out there knows about Space
- The laugh = not quite O.T.T.O.'s voice, warmer, older - raises questions about who is really speaking
- Relief + parental pride = suggests O.T.T.O. (or whoever is laughing) was waiting for this moment

### O.T.T.O.'s Fragmented Memory (general)
- Memory is damaged/corrupted
- Recognizes certain Spelljammer symbols, names, or tech - but doesn't know why
- Later reveal: can project a star chart when partially repaired at the Academy

### Potential Academy Discoveries (reveal when ready):
- Old records mentioning "Rivet" or a Kenku artificer
- M.E.R.L.I.N. (or a fragment) in storage/decommissioned
- An NPC who knew Rivet but hasn't seen him in decades
- Tech compatible with O.T.T.O. that could unlock more memories
