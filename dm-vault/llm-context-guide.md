# LLM Context Guide - The Wild Expanse

This document provides context for future LLMs assisting with worldbuilding, campaign planning, and character development for The Wild Expanse campaign.

---

## Campaign Overview

- **Name:** The Wild Expanse
- **System:** D&D 5e with Wildjammer rules (Spelljammer setting)
- **Starting Adventure:** Spelljammer Academy (prologue module)
- **Tone:** High adventure, ancient magic, the endless Astral Sea

### Core Setting Concept
The Wild Expanse is a universe where wooden galleons sail among the stars, powered by arcane Spelljamming Helms. The void is alive - a breathable ocean of aether and starlight. This is fantasy space, not sci-fi.

### Key Cosmology
- **Wildspace:** Void between celestial bodies within a solar system
- **Crystal Spheres:** Supermassive shells encasing each solar system
- **The Phlogiston:** Chaotic rainbow ocean between Crystal Spheres (highly flammable, cuts off planar access)
- **Astral Sea:** Connects planes of existence; home to gods and Githyanki

---

## DM Style & Preferences

### Working Style
- **Breadcrumb Approach:** Plant seeds now, decide exact answers later. Avoid writing into corners.
- **Long-term arcs:** Character stories are subplots that simmer in the background across multiple adventures.
- **Collaborative:** Open to brainstorming but prefers focused, one-topic-at-a-time discussion.

### Creative Preferences
- Dislikes formulaic or "uninspired" suggestions - push for creativity
- Prefers mystery over explanation - leave questions for players to chew on
- Values emotional resonance ("tug at the heartstrings")
- No emojis in documents

### Communication Style
- Direct feedback ("I don't like that", "be more creative")
- Prefers to stay focused on one thread at a time
- Will tell you when to pause or switch topics
- Uses numbered options for quick selection (responds with "1", "2", etc.)

## Working With This DM

1. **Stay focused** - one topic at a time
2. **Be creative** - avoid generic/formulaic suggestions
3. **Leave mystery** - don't over-explain; let things simmer
4. **Update documents** - capture decisions in the relevant files
5. **Use the brainstorm docs** - they track progress and TODOs
6. **Ask before assuming** - prefer targeted questions over shotgun approaches
